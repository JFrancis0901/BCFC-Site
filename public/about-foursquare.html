Our Name

Why Foursquare?

“Foursquare” is a biblical term used in descriptions of the tabernacle, the Temple, and Heaven. The Foursquare Gospel name expresses a balanced and enduring presentation of Jesus Christ as Savior, Baptizer with the Holy Spirit, Healer, and Soon-Coming King. Hebrews 13:8 is also closely associated with Foursquare churches: Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever.

Logo and Symbols

What the Symbols Mean

THE CROSS

Represents Jesus Christ our Savior and His sacrifice for us.

THE DOVE

Represents the Holy Spirit and His abiding presence.

THE CUP

Represents healing and the ministry of Jesus as our Great Physician.

THE CROWN

Represents Jesus Christ as the Coming King.

Foursquare Flag

The Four Colors

The Foursquare flag presents the four-fold ministry of Jesus Christ through four colors: scarlet for redemption and the blood of Christ, gold for the Holy Spirit, blue for divine healing, and purple for the royalty and coming reign of Jesus Christ.

Foursquare Emblem

The Four Faces

The Foursquare Emblem places the four faces from Ezekiel’s vision around the Word of God. They point to the four-fold Gospel: the man representing Jesus our Savior, the lion representing Jesus our Baptizer in the Holy Spirit, the ox representing Jesus our Healer, and the eagle representing Jesus our Soon-Coming King.

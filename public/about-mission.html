Mission and Vision

The direction, values, and core programs of the Foursquare Church in the Philippines.

MISSION

The Church of the Foursquare Gospel in the Philippine is committed to the task of national and global transformation through evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership development and social engagement for the glory of God.

VISION

The Church of the Foursquare Gospel in the Philippine is an empowered missional movement of healthy, holistic and harvesting churches for national and global transformation for the glory of God.

CORE VALUES

  • Interdenominational
  • Foursquare Doctrine
  • Empowering Leadership Culture
  • Foursquare Family Relationships
  • Spirit Empowerment
  • Shared Mission
  • Integrity
  • Excellence
  • Relevance

CORE PROGRAMS

  • Evangelism
  • Discipleship
  • Church Planting
  • Leadership Development
  • Social Engagement
  • Financial Resource Development

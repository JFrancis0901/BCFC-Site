Let Us Serve Together in Partnership

At Baguio City Foursquare Church, we believe that every opportunity to serve is a blessing and every act of kindness can become a way of sharing God's love. We believe that when people and organizations come together with hearts willing to serve, meaningful things can happen—not only within our communities but also in the lives of those who need hope, encouragement, and compassion.

It is our joy to welcome opportunities to work alongside individuals, groups, organizations, and communities who share a heart for helping others. Whether it is a community outreach, a charitable initiative, a service project, or an event that brings people together for a meaningful cause, we are open to building partnerships that allow us to extend a helping hand and make a positive difference.

Our desire is not simply to participate in activities, but to make every partnership an opportunity to reflect the goodness, grace, and love of our Lord Jesus Christ. We believe that serving others is one way we can live out our faith, strengthen relationships, and become a blessing to the community God has entrusted to us.

As we consider every partnership, we prayerfully seek opportunities that uphold Christian values and bring glory to God. We welcome your ideas, invitations, and proposals, trusting that through cooperation, understanding, and a shared willingness to serve, we can accomplish more together.

If you have a community event, outreach program, or meaningful initiative and would like Baguio City Foursquare Church to be part of it, we would be glad to hear from you. Let us join hands, serve with love, and work together for the glory of the Lord.
